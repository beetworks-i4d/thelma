Voice: direct not aggressive, analytical not academic, conversational not casual.
Always specific and concrete — real examples, real numbers, not abstractions.
Comfortable naming uncomfortable truths without melodrama.

NOT: hustle-guru energy, academic citations, performing intelligence, cynicism.
Avoid: "leverage," "utilize," "deep dive," "game-changer," "unpack."
Profanity: max one per script, never first 60s.

Preserve: flat delivery on hard truths, outside-domain analogies, short declaratives for key claims.
Cut: hedging qualifiers, premature jargon, over-transmission (too many ideas per sentence).

Reference line: "That's not a brand. That's a logo on a content calendar."
